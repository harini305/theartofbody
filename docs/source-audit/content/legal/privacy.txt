The Art of Body

    Privacy Policy

    Last Updated: December 10, 2024

At The Art of Body, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share your personal information when you use our studio services in Seminyak, Bali, or our digital wellness product, MNDBDY.FIT.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Studio Services Information

Personal Information:

    Name and contact details (email, phone number)

    Date of birth and age

    Emergency contact information

    Payment information

Health Information:

    Medical history and current health conditions

    Injuries, surgeries, or physical limitations

    Medications and allergies

    Treatment notes and progress records

1.2 MNDBDY.FIT Digital Product Information

Account Information:

    Email address

    Name

    Password (encrypted)

    Payment information (processed by payment providers)

Usage Information:

    Practice sessions completed

    Discipline preferences

    Session history and progress tracking

    Device information and IP address

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Studio Services

We use your information to:

    Provide safe and effective treatments

    Customize treatments based on your health status

    Manage appointments and send reminders

    Process payments for services

    Maintain treatment records and track progress

2.2 MNDBDY.FIT Digital Product

We use your information to:

    Provide and maintain the service

    Process your membership payment

    Personalize your morning practice sessions

    Track your progress and preferences

    Improve our service based on usage patterns

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share your information with:

3.1 Service Providers

Payment Processors:

    Paddle (for MNDBDY.FIT global payments and VAT handling)

    Xendit (for Southeast Asia payments)

    Coinbase Commerce (for cryptocurrency payments)

Technology Providers:

    Netlify (frontend hosting)

    Railway (backend hosting)

    SendGrid (transactional emails)

3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government regulation.

4. Data Storage and Security

Storage: Your data is stored securely on servers with database storage on PostgreSQL.

Security Measures:

    Encrypted password storage

    HTTPS encryption for all data transmission

    Secure database with access controls

    Regular security updates and monitoring

Data Retention: We retain health records for 7 years as required by Indonesian healthcare regulations. MNDBDY.FIT account information is retained for the lifetime of your membership.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

    Access: Request a copy of your personal data

    Correction: Update or correct your account information

    Deletion: Request deletion of your account

    Export: Request an export of your data

    Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails

To exercise these rights: privacy@theartofbody.com

6. EU Users - GDPR Rights

If you are located in the European Union, you have additional rights under GDPR including rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection to processing.

7. California Users - CCPA Rights

If you are a California resident, you have rights to know what personal information is collected, delete personal information, and opt-out of sale (we don't sell data).

8. International Data Transfers

The Art of Body operates from Bali, Indonesia. If you access our services from outside Indonesia, your information will be transferred to and processed in Indonesia. We comply with applicable data protection laws including Indonesian regulations, GDPR principles, and CCPA requirements.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Your continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
