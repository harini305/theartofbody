    Refund & Cancellation Policy

    Last Updated: December 10, 2024

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains our policies for studio services and our digital product, MNDBDY.FIT. Please read this policy carefully before making a purchase.

1. Studio Services - Refund Policy

1.1 Single Session Payments

Completed Sessions: Individual session payments are non-refundable once the service has been provided.

1.2 Session Packages

Non-Refundable: All prepaid session packages are non-refundable after purchase.

Transferable: Unused sessions may be transferred to another person with prior written approval.

1.3 Special Circumstances

Refunds may be considered in exceptional circumstances:

    Medical Emergency: Serious illness or injury (medical documentation required)

    Relocation: Permanent move away from Bali (proof required)

    Service Not Delivered: If we cannot provide the service

2. Studio Services - Cancellation Policy

2.1 Appointment Cancellations

        Notice Period

        Policy

        24+ hours notice

        Free cancellation or rescheduling

        Less than 24 hours

        Session fee charged

        No-show

        Full session fee charged

2.2 How to Cancel

To cancel or reschedule:

    Email: info@theartofbody.com

    In person at the studio

3. MNDBDY.FIT - Refund Policy

    IMPORTANT: All MNDBDY.FIT Founder's Edition purchases are final and non-refundable due to the digital nature of the product and immediate access granted upon purchase.

3.1 No Refunds Policy

Why No Refunds: MNDBDY.FIT is a digital product. Upon payment, you receive immediate lifetime access to all content. Once access credentials are provided, the product has been delivered.

3.2 Technical Issues Exception

If you experience technical problems preventing access:

    Report within 7 days: Contact support@mndbdy.fit

    Troubleshooting: We will work to resolve issues

    Refund consideration: Only if we cannot resolve after reasonable efforts

Technical issues do NOT include:

    Change of mind after purchase

    Personal device compatibility

    Internet connectivity issues

    User error

4. Payment Processing

4.1 Payment Providers

We use secure payment processors:

    Paddle: Global payments (handles VAT for EU customers)

    Xendit: Southeast Asia payments

    Coinbase Commerce: Cryptocurrency

4.2 Chargeback Policy

Before initiating a chargeback: Please contact us first. We will work with you to resolve issues.

Chargeback consequences:

    MNDBDY.FIT access will be immediately suspended

    You may be banned from future purchases

    We reserve the right to dispute illegitimate chargebacks

5. Founder's Edition Special Terms

5.1 Limited Availability

The Founder's Edition is limited to the first 100 members. Once sold out, future pricing will be annual subscription at $297/year.

5.2 Lifetime Access Definition

"Lifetime Access" means:

    Access for as long as the service operates

    No recurring fees ever

    Access to all future content updates

    Access continues even if pricing models change

6. Refund Process

6.1 How to Request a Refund

For eligible refund requests:

    Email appropriate contact:

            Studio: info@theartofbody.com

            MNDBDY.FIT: support@mndbdy.fit

    Include: name, purchase date, amount, reason, supporting documents

    Wait for review (5 business days response time)

6.2 Refund Timeline

If approved:

    Processing: 3-5 business days

    Return to original payment method

    Bank processing: 5-10 business days (varies by bank)
