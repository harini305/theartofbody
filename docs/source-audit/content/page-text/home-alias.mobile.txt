Our Story

The Art of Body is a professional Pilates and bodywork studio in Seminyak, Bali.

Since 2009, we've specialized in therapeutic movement practices that combine STOTT Pilates training with hands-on bodywork techniques including myofascial release and Bowen therapy.

What Makes Us Different

- STOTT-trained Pilates instruction focused on rehabilitation and pain management

- Small group classes (maximum 3 people) for personalized attention  
- Private one-on-one sessions tailored to your specific needs
- Therapeutic bodywork including Bowen technique and myofascial release
- 15+ years of experience helping clients achieve pain-free movement

Whether you're recovering from injury, managing chronic pain, or looking to improve your core strength and posture, our personalized approach ensures you receive the care and attention you deserve.

Located in the heart of Seminyak, Bali, we serve both local residents and wellness travelers from around the world.
