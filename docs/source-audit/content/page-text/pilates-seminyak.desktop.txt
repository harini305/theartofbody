PILATES SEMINYAK

at The Art of Body

is a STOTT trained professional Pilates Studio in Bali. Since 2009 we have catered to small group or private settings to enrich our client's wellbeing.

From core stability, spinal rehabilitation and toning the body, we create a tailor-made service to each client.

We thrive in supporting your process by teaching you proper technique, by executing careful designed programs and adding a unique twist to your routine each time you come in.

Back Rehabilitation • Core Stabilisation • Postural Problems • Muscular and Joint instability • Chronic Injury Rehabilitation  • Toning and Lengthening • Post- Baby toning   

PILATES IN SEMINYAK OFFERINGS

Our pilates in Seminyak offerings range from consultations to full, week-long pilates retreats. 

PROFESSIONAL INITIAL CONSULTATION   (1 HOUR)

Rp. 750.000

Your first consultation will identify your postural needs, your body's imbalances and your personal goals in your journey in pilates. In this one hour session, you will be assessed by a STOTT certified professional so we can write a tailor-made program for you. Postural analysis, strength testings and correct form will be discussed and tested appropriately.

PRIVATE PILATES   (1 HOUR)

Rp. 750.000   /   3pack  Rp.2.050.000  /  10 pack  Rp.7.300.000 

Private Pilates is recommend to anyone who wants to take pilates at their own pace. Privates are dedicated in teaching you the fundamentals, the modifications or the advancements of each exercise. The appropriate equipment and program will be written to suit your personal needs and fitness goals.

COUPLES PILATES   (1 HOUR)

Rp.900.000   /   3 pack  Rp.2.550.000   /   10 pack  Rp.8.100.000 

Private Pilates is recommend to anyone who wants to take pilates at their own pace. Privates are dedicated in teaching you the fundamentals, the modifications or the advancements of each exercise. The appropriate equipment and program will be written to suit your personal needs and fitness goals.

GROUP MAT PILATES   (1 HOUR)

Rp. 300.000 per person / minimum 4 pax

Group mat pilates is great for larger organised groups, beginners or maintenance programs. Group mat pilates is appropriate for 4-8 people at one time. Group mat pilates can also be organised for in-villa purposes. Please email us for your most convenient time.
