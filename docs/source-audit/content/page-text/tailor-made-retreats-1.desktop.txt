THE ART OF BODY is a boutique wellness and holistic fitness studio, specialising in pilates retreat in Bali. We focus on personalised health and coaching sessions that go beyond the body to connect with the mind. It is through mind, body and spirit we can truly experience physical change and lifestyle shifts.

Our classes, programs and retreats are designed with practitioners who are delicately chosen to inspire you to feel, and look, your best. From all walks of life, our team is dedicated to welcoming clients from all over the world and different life stages so you'll always feel you have an advocate for change. From classical STOTT pilates classes to catered movement therapies, we are determined for you to stand taller and feel happier with your health and well being.

At THE ART OF BODY, we help you re-define the lines of your body, improve your posture and elongate to feel strong and look lean. Book your one-on-one or small-group class, or if you're looking to explore deeper depths of body movement therapy, see our women's wellness and dance retreats.
